About Dr. Arushi Goel

Dr. Arushi Goel is a dedicated dental professional committed to delivering comprehensive, ethical and personalised dental care. With a strong academic foundation and diverse clinical experience, she combines clinical expertise, precision and a compassionate approach to dentistry.

She completed her postgraduation in 2023, complemented by specialised clinical training at Christian Medical College (CMC), Vellore, where she gained extensive exposure to holistic and hospital-based dentistry, including the comprehensive dental management of medically compromised patients and individuals with complex healthcare needs.

Following her postgraduate training, she served at Mahatma Gandhi District Hospital, Madhya Pradesh, contributing to public healthcare delivery and community dental services.

As the driving force behind The Meraki Dental, her vision is to create a welcoming, modern dental practice where quality treatment meets genuine care. She believes dentistry goes beyond treating dental problems — it is about understanding individual needs, building confidence and ensuring long-term oral health.

Her philosophy is simple: Care. Precision. Confidence.
